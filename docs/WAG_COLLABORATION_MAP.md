## 1. The broader strategic lesson this doc sits inside

**WAG should become an entertainment universe capable of introducing personalities, formats, franchises, and IP — not simply a collection of YouTube channels.** (Katie, 2026-09-10, drawn from the LOL Podcast/Last Laugh competitive study.) Recurring collaborators, serialized relationships, and audience-transfer paths are how a real entertainment universe actually expands — this doc's framework is the operational layer under that strategic lesson, not the lesson itself. Keep this framing visible whenever this doc gets extended.

## 2. The core principle this doc organizes around

**Audience transfers through participation, not promotion.** (Source: 2026-09-10 LOL Podcast/Last Laugh competitive deep-dive — see that report's full evidence and `WAG_LOL_PODCAST_COLLABORATION_CARD.md` §0A.) A guest who actually appears inside WAG's own content, repeatedly, earning real audience investment over time, converts audience at a materially higher rate than a channel simply mentioning or cross-promoting someone. This is why the framework below is built around **recurring characters and serialized relationships**, not one-time guest slots — a one-time appearance is closer to "promotion"; a real arc is "participation."

**This does not replace WAG's existing multi-factor diagnosis of WAG Podcast's growth problem.** Packaging/CTR, the recommendation neighborhood, Shorts-to-long-form conversion, and first-30-second retention/format all remain independently real and relevant — see `WAG_ALGORITHM_DIAGNOSTIC.md`, `WAG_PACKAGING_INTELLIGENCE.md`, and `WAG_MEASUREMENT_STANDARDS.md`. Recurring-collaborator audience transfer is one more real lever, evaluated on its own evidence, not a claim that it's the missing piece.

## 3. The recurring-character framework

For every strong candidate, work through:

**FIRST APPEARANCE** — the real, specific, lowest-commitment entry point (a single real episode/segment, grounded in something both parties already genuinely do — never an invented format).
**SECOND APPEARANCE / ARC** — what would make a real return appearance earn its place, i.e. what unresolved thread, callback, or genuine ongoing relationship justifies bringing them back rather than treating it as a one-off.
**SHORTS OPPORTUNITY** — how the collaboration produces real, separable short-form moments (per LOL Podcast's own evidence, their highest-multiplier Shorts were dual-attributed collab clips, not solo content).
**MAIN OR PODCAST FIT** — which brand this genuinely belongs to, per the permanent two-site identity split (Main WAG = adventure/mystery/paranormal/extreme-content; WAG Podcast = conversation/relationships/opinions/community). Never blend the two brands' voice for a single candidate.
**AUDIENCE-TRANSFER PATH** — the concrete mechanism by which the collaborator's own audience would actually discover and follow WAG content (a real cited reach number, not an assumption of goodwill).

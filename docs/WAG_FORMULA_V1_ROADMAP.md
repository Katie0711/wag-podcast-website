## Packaging philosophy, applied

**Titles** above default to flat, declarative, quantified statements of the real stake — the strongest cross-creator default (see Formula v1 Question 6) — except #7, where a real, unresolved question ("Real or Fake?") is the honest hook, per WAG's own Rule-of-3-confirmed evidence that question-framing works through a different, real mechanism (the Zeigarnik effect) when the actual hook is ambiguity rather than a claim to test.

**Thumbnails**, corrected per Formula v1 Question 6: genuine, unrestrained real reactions — including open-mouthed, visibly shocked or disgusted real expressions — are not something to avoid. WAG's own real highest-performing thumbnail ("EVERY WORST Rated Restaurant") is exactly that. The actual rule is genuine-vs-staged, not restrained-vs-exaggerated: never a forced or exaggerated expression staged for the thumbnail rather than pulled from a real moment.

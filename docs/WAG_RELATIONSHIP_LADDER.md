# WAG Relationship Ladder

**LAST VERIFIED:** 2026-08-15
**VERSION:** v1.0 canonical — reconciled from "WAG-Relationship-Ladder.pdf" (WAG Growth Project, Phase 5, Revised V3, Aug 2026). Separate but connected system from `WAG_CONCEPT_PACKAGING_SYSTEM.md`.
**OWNER:** Owned Audience/Commerce Director
**CORE PRINCIPLE:** The real asset isn't the email address — it's the permission. Participation leads to email, doesn't replace it. Every interaction is designed with a real email outcome built in, not bolted on after.

**Standing test for every interaction:** (1) Why would they participate? (2) Why would they give permission to stay in touch? (3) Why would they come back next week? Answering all three = worth building. Answering only one = incomplete, not wrong.

## Flagged decision — not resolved here, carried forward from the source document

**Anonymous/unmoderated question-submission was proposed and explicitly flagged as a real minors-safety risk**, not a hypothetical one: an open, anonymous inbox from a mostly-teen audience has a real path to self-harm disclosures or one kid naming/targeting another real kid — content that shouldn't reach an episode script unfiltered and shouldn't sit unread either. **Recommended alternative:** a moderated submission form, same real participation payoff ("your question might get read on air"), reviewed before anything reaches an episode, with no promise of anonymity that isn't true. This is Katie's call, not decided here — see the company decision queue.

## COPPA-conscious by design — permanent constraints on every rung

Minimum information always (email only, no birthdate/phone/address "just in case"). Clear, simple consent language, no dark patterns, no pre-checked boxes. Say what actually happens ("your question might get read on air," not "confess anonymously" — the promise made has to be the promise kept). Moderate before public, every time, no exceptions for convenience. Applies to every rung, not just Join — polls/quizzes are already low-risk (no PII beyond a click); question-submission and anything collecting real content from real minors gets the full standard.

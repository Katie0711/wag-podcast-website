# WAG Content Bucket Framework + Idea Generator

**Part of the WAG hit-making system project (2026-08-08).** Scope: `@TheWildAdventureGirls`, modern-teen-era content only — same boundary as the Channel Publishing QA rollout (everything newer than the Mar 2025 horse-content pivot). Per Katie's directive: **a handful of repeatable buckets, each with room for hundreds of future videos — not dozens of random one-off ideas.** This doc owns the buckets and the idea-generation mechanic; `WAG_STORYTELLING_SYSTEM.md` owns how an idea becomes an episode once picked; `WAG_VIDEO_GREENLIGHT.md` owns whether it gets filmed.

**Production constraint, explicit and load-bearing:** ideas here are constrained to realistic Texas-based production — no assumed celebrity collabs, no beaches, no constant travel. Per Katie's framing, this is a creative advantage, not a limitation: it forces buckets that can genuinely sustain hundreds of episodes on repeatable, local production, rather than a handful of expensive one-offs.

## The buckets (4 real, 1 explicitly flagged, not 5 equally weighted)

### 1. Viral-Testing (Texas-based, zero travel required)

**Real prior art (3 of 10 sampled videos):** Trying EVERY Viral TikTok Product On the Internet!, I Tested EVERY Viral TikTok Product I Could Buy!, I Ate EVERY Viral TikTok Food In a New City!

**Container:** the completeness-claim pattern confirmed this session (`WAG_PACKAGING_INTELLIGENCE.md`) — "EVERY [category]," tested/eaten/tried, real reactions as the payoff. Swappable variable: the product/food category.

**Why it's a real bucket, not a one-off:** already run 3 times with real, current variables (general viral products, buyable viral products, viral food) — the container tolerates repetition without feeling stale because the *specific products* change every time.

**Room for hundreds of episodes:** yes — new viral products/foods surface constantly and don't require any travel, just purchasing power and a kitchen/living room, both already in place.

### 2. Worst-Rated / Most-Hated Places (Texas-based, local travel only)

**Real prior art (1 of 10 sampled videos):** I Ate at EVERY WORST Rated Restaurant In a New City!

**Container:** the same completeness-claim pattern, applied to real, verifiable low-star-rated local businesses — restaurants, and by extension any category with public review data (attractions, ice cream/dessert spots, escape rooms, mini-golf, etc.).

**Why it's a real bucket, not a one-off:** only one real instance so far, but the container generalizes cleanly to any category with public star ratings, and "new city" doesn't require leaving Texas — most major Texas metro areas have enough low-rated spots in any category to sustain this indefinitely.

**Room for hundreds of episodes:** yes, with one caveat — this bucket needs a light real-world sourcing step each time (checking actual current reviews) to keep the "worst-rated" claim honest, same discipline WAG already holds for content-authenticity.

### 3. Overnight Cryptid / Paranormal Challenges (Texas-based, real regional folklore)

**Real prior art (3 of 10 sampled videos):** 24 Hours in America's Most Bigfoot Sighted Forest!, 24 Hours in America's Most Haunted Hotel! *Ghost in Room*, 24 HOURS in the GOATMAN'S FOREST! (It Followed Us!) — the last of these is WAG's own real Lake Worth Monster content (per the standing `wag_positioning_and_facts` note: WAG's Goatman content is the Lake Worth Monster legend, not the separate Old Alton Bridge/"Goatman's Bridge" legend in Denton).

**Container:** a real, named location tied to a real regional legend, a stated overnight time-box, and the Ryan Trahan-style checkpoint pacing recommended in `WAG_STORYTELLING_SYSTEM.md` (state the plan, hit real checkpoints, close on a real reaction).

**Why it's a real bucket, not a one-off:** run 3 times already with distinct real legends (Bigfoot, a haunted hotel, the Lake Worth Monster/"Goatman"). Texas and nearby regions carry a genuinely deep bench of real, well-documented legends distinct from what's already used — e.g. the Marfa Lights (Marfa, TX), the Bragg Road Ghost Light (Saratoga, TX), the Donkey Lady Bridge (San Antonio, TX), and the separate Old Alton Bridge/"Goatman's Bridge" legend (Denton, TX) that WAG has *not* yet used (confirmed distinct from the Lake Worth content already made). These are named as real, verifiable regional folklore worth researching before filming — not claims about what would happen on camera.

**Room for hundreds of episodes:** realistically dozens, not hundreds, without repeating locations — the bucket is real and strong but has a narrower ceiling than the two testing buckets above; worth tracking real-location supply as a constraint, not treating as infinite.

### 4. Travel/Event Vlogs — flagged, not a growth bucket

**Real prior art (2-3 of 10 sampled videos):** We Rode EVERY Disney Ride in One Day!, Surviving 24 Hours of Embarrassing Challenges at VidCon!, Flying to LA for the Craziest Weekend Ever!

**Real pushback (per the standing hit-making-system directive, not a new judgment call):** the project's own scope notes already flag that "the vlog format itself is already known not to be the direction — study why it underperforms, don't recommend more of it." These 3 videos are real, recent, and already published, but this bucket should be **studied, not expanded** — it also directly conflicts with the Texas-based production constraint above (Disney/VidCon/LA all required travel). Recommend a dedicated retention/performance check on these 3 specific videos (real numbers, not assumed) before any further travel-format content gets greenlit, rather than defaulting to "more of what we already did."

## The idea generator — how a bucket becomes a candidate idea

Each bucket above is a **container with one or two swappable variables** — the same mechanic documented in `WAG_STORYTELLING_SYSTEM.md`'s Episode Framework Library. Generating a new candidate idea means filling the variable slot with something real, not inventing a scenario:

| Bucket | Swappable variable(s) | How to source the variable honestly |
|---|---|---|
| Viral-Testing | Product/food category (skincare, tech gadgets, kitchen tools, back-to-school, seasonal/holiday items) | Real, current TikTok/social trend data at time of filming — never invent a "viral" claim |
| Worst-Rated Places | Business category + city | Real, current public review platforms (Google/Yelp) checked at time of filming |
| Overnight Cryptid | Real, named regional legend + real location | Real, documented regional folklore (see the 4 named above as a starting research list, not a commitment) |

**What this generator does NOT do:** produce a list of "next 10 video ideas" as if they were pre-approved. It produces *candidates* — a bucket + a variable — which then go through `WAG_VIDEO_GREENLIGHT.md` before anything gets treated as a real plan. This matches the standing no-fake-certainty guardrail: raising idea quality and narrowing the field, not guaranteeing outcomes.

**Explicitly not proposed:** a 5th "invented" bucket to round out a nicer-looking list. Katie's directive was a *handful* of buckets with real depth, not an arbitrary round number — 3 strong buckets plus 1 flagged-for-caution bucket is the honest current state of the evidence.

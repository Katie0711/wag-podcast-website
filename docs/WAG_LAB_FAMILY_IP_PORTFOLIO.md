# WAG Lab Family — IP Portfolio Concept

**LAST VERIFIED:** 2026-08-15
**STATUS:** Strategic concept + preliminary screening only. **Every name below is a trademark candidate, not a cleared mark.** Nothing filed, no counsel contacted, no purchase made, no company architecture changed by this document alone.
**OWNER:** Publishing & IP Director

---

## The strategic hypothesis

Larger than one book: a family of proprietary WAG educational/publishing sub-brands — potentially **WAG Slime Lab, WAG Edible Slime Lab, WAG Science Lab, WAG Edible Science Lab, WAG Animal Lab**, and further WAG + category properties as the IP portfolio expands. If a name/format proves out, it could extend across books, video, websites/digital experiences, educational products, kits, membership experiences, and licensing — not just print.

## Real preliminary findings (screening only, not legal clearance)

1. **A live 2026 U.S. trademark application exists for "THE SLIME LAB" in Class 41** (entertainment/education services) — a real, pending, specific legal fact, stronger evidence than the earlier general web-usage screen. This independently reinforces avoiding "Slime Lab" as a core phrase, beyond the product-name collisions already found (SmartLab, Galt, MindWare kits).
2. **"Science Lab" is used descriptively/crowded in educational contexts** — real risk of being found merely descriptive/hard to enforce even if not directly blocked by one specific mark.
3. **Wag Labs, Inc. already holds real U.S. trademark rights around WAG!/WAG-related pet products and services.** This is the most consequential finding for this specific naming family: **"WAG Labs" and "WAG Animal Lab" carry a direct, serious collision risk** — not just a similar-sounding name, but an existing company already using "WAG" in a registered context, and "Animal Lab" sits close to their actual category (pet-related).

**Revision to the naming work delivered minutes ago:** my own top recommendation, "Blorp's Slime Lab," still contains "Slime Lab" as its core phrase and inherits the real Class 41 application risk found here — flagging this explicitly rather than letting an already-stated recommendation stand uncorrected. "WAG Slime Science" avoids "Lab" and "Slime Lab" entirely, which now reads as the stronger of the two candidates already proposed, though it still needs the same formal clearance before any commitment.

## Permanent Publishing/IP workflow (added 2026-08-15)

**Concept → market validation → naming → preliminary trademark screen → attorney clearance → Katie approval → filing decision → development → launch → performance → IP expansion decision.**

No WAG publishing/IP concept proceeds past "naming" without a preliminary screen, and no name is filed or publicly committed without attorney clearance and Katie's explicit approval. This is now the standing gate for every future book/property in this family, not a one-time step for the slime book alone.

## IP Expansion Trigger (added 2026-08-15, permanent)

Whenever a WAG concept performs strongly, Publishing/IP evaluates whether it can become: books, digital experiences, educational products, merchandise/kits, licensing, entertainment formats, or another defensible WAG property. This formalizes and extends the existing IP-expansion review already in `WAG_COMPANY_BLUEPRINT.md` §7 ("what else can this become?") — same principle, now explicitly tied to the Lab-family naming workflow above so a strong performer has a defined path to becoming its own cleared, named property rather than an ad hoc rename.

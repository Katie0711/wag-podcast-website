## How to read this

Business engines (`WAG_COMPANY_BLUEPRINT.md` §2) are what WAG monetizes *through*. This map shows the same 18 previously-inventoried capabilities organized under the 5-Director structure (Blueprint §4), each with its accountable Director. Cross-cutting functions serve every Director/engine regardless of which Director is accountable for their ownership/prioritization — see Blueprint §4's 2026-08-15 resolution.

## Media / WAG Main

**Chain, restated per Katie's explicit correction (2026-08-15): capability existence is not operating maturity.** The real Definition of Done is the loop actually closing and changing the next bet — **DETECT → RESEARCH → UNDERSTAND → RECOMMEND → DECIDE → EXECUTE → MEASURE → LEARN → CHANGE THE NEXT BET** — not the existence of a research library, however sophisticated. Applied to Main: Idea → Research (WAG internal truth × world-class creator intelligence × current platform intelligence) → Greenlight → Predict → Package → Produce → Publish → Measure (exposure/CTR/retention/distribution/audience/fandom/economics) → Diagnose why → Intervene → Outcome → Learn → Change next decision.

**Real position today:** through Predict for 6 of 10 Next-10 ideas; nothing has closed the loop past Publish for any of the 6. The Haunted Hotel/Bigfoot case is the one real completed loop reaching Learn. **Skinwalker is the live proof case for this exact loop** — DETECT (real audience/demand signal) → RESEARCH (three-lens intelligence, `WAG_MAIN_PROOF_1_SKINWALKER_PREP.md`) → UNDERSTAND (cultural-treatment recommendation, filming-duration honesty) → RECOMMEND (Top 3 package) is done; DECIDE/EXECUTE/MEASURE/LEARN/CHANGE-NEXT-BET are the real remaining stages, pending Katie's creative approval and the actual shoot.

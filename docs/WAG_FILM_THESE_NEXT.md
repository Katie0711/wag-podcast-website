# Copying vs. Format Transfer — WAG Creative Team Reference

Use this before greenlighting any idea that's clearly "inspired by" an outside creator's video. The test: are we borrowing the *mechanism*, or their *specific execution*?

## What legitimately transfers

**Premise structure — stated rule in the first seconds, no ambiguity.**
Ryan Trahan's "I Tested 1-Star Restaurants" states the rule by 0:24 ("I have to leave a 5 star review at every restaurant"). WAG's "I Ate at EVERY WORST Rated Restaurant" already does this natively ("The Mission: Testing the Worst-Rated Spots," 0:00-0:45). Any new WAG idea should open the same way.

**Stakes as a hard number, not a vague duration.**
MrBeast's Circle format states "Last To Leave... Wins $500,000" — a number, not "until we're done." WAG's "24 Hours in America's Most Haunted Hotel" mirrors this with its stated "3 Questions We Came to Answer" device.

**Engineered escalation (the rule gets harder, not just longer).**
MrBeast's Circle physically shrinks over days rather than just running out a clock. WAG's Commitment adaptations (e.g., stacking "My Sister Decides" from 24 hours to 7 days) should escalate the actual constraint each day, not just repeat day 1 seven times.

**Title construction — duration/number stated explicitly, every time.**
Airrack's "#195" badge, MrBeast's "DAY 6" burned into the thumbnail, Trahan's franchise naming — all state the scope up front. WAG's own "I Ate at EVERY WORST Rated Restaurant In a New City!" already follows this; keep doing it.

**Thumbnail psychology — genuine reaction + visible checklist of the claim.**
Nick DiGiovanni's flag row, SIS vs BRO's logo row, WAG's own confirmed rule (genuine disgusted expression, not staged-calm) on its top performer. Any new thumbnail should show the scope of the claim visually, not just a face.

**Payoff structure — honest resolution, including "we don't know."**
WAG's Goatman/Hotel investigations close by directly answering the question they opened with. This is WAG's own already-proven DNA (claim -> test -> resolve) — it transfers to any new format wrapper (Commitment, Confinement, Transformation) without changing WAG's voice at all.

**Stacking a proven WAG hook as a "distraction" layer.**
Jordan Matter's pink-room video stacked a color challenge on top of the room reveal and outperformed the single-thread version (48M vs 29M). WAG can legitimately stack its own proven Viral-Testing engine on top of a Transformation reveal the same way — reusing a mechanism that already has real evidence behind it, applied to a new wrapper.

## What should NOT be copied

**A creator's specific recurring cast/ensemble.**
Jordan Matter's 3-vs-3 friend group (Salish, Elliana, Gio, etc.) took years of audience familiarity to build. WAG has 3 people, not two pre-built teams — don't force a scoreboard split that doesn't exist.

**A creator's branded continuity device.**
The "Matter Cup" trophy is Jordan Matter's own channel-specific IP. WAG shouldn't invent an equivalent branded trophy/belt just because the mechanism (head-to-head) transferred.

**Budget/scale that can't be honestly replicated.**
MrBeast's 100-person cast and $500K prize (Circle), or his custom-built padded solitary-confinement set with an on-call psychologist. Faking the scale would break WAG's honesty-first brand — the mechanism (one stated rule + a bounded space) transfers; the production budget does not.

**A creator's real outside-access/credibility source.**
FaZe Rug's video opens with a real invitation from Zach Bagans, a named paranormal investigator with his own museum. WAG doesn't have an equivalent gatekeeper and shouldn't fabricate one — this is exactly why WAG's own Goatman follow-up underperformed its Hotel predecessor 15x; access/credibility isn't a free transfer.

**A performed, non-literal version of a claim.**
Airrack's "every country" is a studio-built, catered representation — he isn't literally traveling anywhere. WAG's honesty-first brand requires "every X" claims to be literally true as filmed, which is exactly why the failed "EVERY Viral TikTok Product" videos (1.7K views) can't be fixed by copying Airrack's staged spectacle — they need a real, checkable local category instead.

**Manufactured conflict between the two minor cast members.**
Any head-to-head format that puts Angelina/Annabella (siblings, one a minor) on opposing scoreboard teams risks reading as manufactured sibling rivalry — a real risk WAG's own prior research flags and hasn't cleared. Jordan Matter's boys-vs-girls framing works because it's not siblings; a direct WAG copy onto the two sisters is not automatically safe.

**Someone else's specific bad review or business name.**
Trahan's exact review text/location is his own sourced content. WAG needs its own freshly-sourced, real local reviews each time — recycling someone else's isn't format transfer, it's copying content.

**Staged "malfunctions" or crisis beats.**
FaZe Rug's claimed on-camera equipment malfunction as a dramatic capstone only works if genuinely real — manufacturing an equivalent breaks WAG's real-reactions-only rule and its no-scripted-plot constraint.

---

**Quick gut-check before greenlighting:** if the idea only works because of someone else's budget, cast history, outside access, or staged beat — it's copying. If it works because of a rule, a number, an escalation shape, a title formula, or an honest ending — it's format transfer, and it's fair game.

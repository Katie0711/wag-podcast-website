### Thumbnail science — what's actually real
- **Well-lit + larger face → real, quantified lift.** `STRONG CROSS-CREATOR EVIDENCE` — Vevo's 2018 study (3,972 videos): 12% average view lift in the first 20 days; Halsey's "Ghost" saw a 4,000% two-week spike after a dark→bright thumbnail swap. The single best real before/after natural experiment found in either research pass.
- **YouTube's own Test & Compare tool optimizes for watch time, not raw CTR — anti-clickbait by design.** `PLATFORM PRINCIPLE`.
- **Exaggerated "shocked face" thumbnails are not a safe default — real evidence of both a documented reversal and real audience fatigue.** `STRONG CROSS-CREATOR EVIDENCE` — MrBeast's own 2023 A/B test found a closed-mouth (less exaggerated) expression beating the open-mouth "shocked" convention his team had standardized on; The Ringer's reporting independently documents real audience fatigue with exaggerated "YouTube Face." **Direct correction to this doc's earlier thumbnail instinct**: genuine reaction, not exaggerated performance.
- **"Redundant title+thumbnail underperforms a complementary pairing"** — `HYPOTHESIS`, not proven. Widely repeated, extends real academic curiosity-gap theory (Loewenstein), but zero traceable controlled evidence found. Used here as a reasoned bet, not a settled rule.

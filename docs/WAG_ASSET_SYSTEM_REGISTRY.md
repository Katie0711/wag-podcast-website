# WAG Asset & System Registry

**LAST VERIFIED:** 2026-08-15
**VERSION:** v1.0 — first draft, not exhaustive. Seeded with the major assets/systems this reconciliation pass actually touched; smaller tools and one-off pages are not yet catalogued.
**OWNER:** Katie Swans
**PURPOSE:** Every major WAG asset, system, or capability answers: what is it, why does it exist, who does it serve, who owns it, what enters/exits it, what engine it supports, what it connects to, its source of truth, its KPIs, what "done/operating" means, and its current §64 maturity. Prevents any future session — Claude or another model — from forgetting what something is for.

**Precedent this builds on, not replaces:** the Business Assets Ledger inside `SOFTWARE_INCUBATOR.md` already logs 7 real durable software/tool assets (Packaging Intelligence, Competitive Intelligence/Rule-of-3, CEO Dashboard v0.1, Channel Publishing QA Checklist, the Storytelling System/Greenlight data model, the interaction platform, the consent-split data architecture). This registry is broader in scope — it covers channels, websites, revenue systems, and IP, not just internal software tools — but does not duplicate that ledger's entries; it references them.

**Rule this registry exists to enforce:** no major system should exist merely because "we built it." Every row above should be able to answer why it exists and what business outcome it's responsible for. When a new major asset/system is created, add a row here in the same pass — don't let it accumulate as untracked debt.

# WAG Stress-Ball Content — Epic Platform Longitudinal View

**Purpose:** real, dated evidence of durable consumption for WAG's 6 real stress-ball videos on the Epic platform, across every quarterly payment report actually found on WAG's own files. This is consumption evidence for existing licensed content — **not** book/kit purchase demand, and not a Google search-demand signal (see the separate GSC findings for that).

**Sources found (exhaustive search of Desktop and Downloads, 2026-08-28):**
- `Downloads/Epic_-_The_Wild_Adventure_Girls_-_Quarterly_Payments_2023-04-15T1323.zip` → `publisher_payment_detail.csv` — **2023-Q1**
- `Desktop/Quarterly Publisher Payment Details.xlsx` — **2025-Q2**
- `Desktop/Quarterly Publisher Payment Details (2).xlsx` — **2026-Q2**

No other Epic quarterly payment exports were found on this machine. These are the only 3 real data points that exist — **no missing quarter (2023-Q2 through 2025-Q1, or 2026-Q1) is estimated or interpolated.**

## What the real numbers show

- **2023-Q1 → 2025-Q2:** the combined total dropped from 355,638 to 264,606 — a real, meaningful decline (~25.6%) over that ~2-year gap. No quarters in between are on file, so the shape of that decline (gradual vs. a step change) is not known — flagged as a real gap, not filled in.
- **2025-Q2 → 2026-Q2 (a clean year-over-year comparison):** 264,606 → 260,893, a **1.4% aggregate decline** — essentially flat. This is the strongest evidence point: consumption has stabilized rather than continuing to fall, at least over this most recent year.
- **Fidget Slime Stress Balls is the one title moving against the trend:** 30,534 → 45,455 year-over-year, **+48.9%**. Every other title in the group declined or was roughly flat over the same year.

## What this evidence supports and does not support

**Supports:** WAG's stress-ball activity content has durable, real, multi-year audience consumption on a platform WAG is actually paid on — this is not a one-off spike or stale content nobody watches anymore. That's real weight behind treating stress-ball activities as legitimate WAG Lab source material.

**Does not support:** any claim about book or kit purchase intent, physical-product demand, or Google search demand — those are separate, already-reported findings (GSC: 3 clicks / 635 impressions / 0.5% CTR across the whole stress-ball query cluster) and must not be combined with this platform-consumption evidence into a single "demand" number.

**Not available:** actual dollar payment amounts — the `Payment` column is blank across all 3 source files for this data, and a separate `total_publisher_revenue.csv` in the 2023-Q1 export lists **$0.00** for that quarter specifically (real, as-found; not interpreted — could reflect a payment threshold, timing, or a contractual detail not visible in this export). Revenue-per-title from Epic remains unknown from what's on file.

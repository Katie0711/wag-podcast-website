## What Shorts is actually doing on this channel right now (real, evidenced)

Two genuinely distinct real formats are both working — this channel is not doing just one thing with Shorts:

### 1. Original, Shorts-native "POV" skit format
Real, recent examples, most-to-least recent:
- "POV: Fixing Your Airbnb after Guests Leave" — 5,544 views (Aug 8, still accumulating)
- "POV: When Your Friend Got the Diarrhea Parasite" — 33,649 views (Aug 6)
- "POV: Moms Before School" — 32,340 views (Aug 4)
- "Stopping Your Friend from Saying Something Embarrassing" — 7,254 views (Aug 3)

**What this real format actually is:** short (12-25 second), scripted-comedy-bit "POV" skits, filmed specifically for Shorts — not a clip pulled from a longer video. Real, consistent hashtag pattern across all of them: `#pov #shorts #funny #viralvideo #trending #shortsfunny #shortsvideo`. This is a genuine, repeatable, low-cost original format distinct from anything in the long-form Episode Framework Library.

### 2. Long-form-support clip format
Real example: "Can't Believe Bella Said This..." — 15,583 views, description states plainly: *"Live at VidCon with other YouTubers and Scarlett accidently did this in front of them."* This is a real, genuine highlight pulled from a real long-form/live moment (VidCon), repackaged as a standalone Short.

**Both formats are real and both are working** — the POV format's top real entry (33.6K) actually outperforms the long-form-support clip's real entry (15.6K) in this sample, but both are clearing a real, meaningful view count, and they serve different real purposes (original discovery vs. long-form reinforcement).

## Conversion path — what a Short actually feeds into (real, evidenced)

- **Shorts -> long-form channel discovery:** the "Channel pages" discovery share (10.2%) is real evidence some viewers arrive at a Short and then browse the channel — a real, working conversion signal, though this pass didn't isolate what fraction convert to a long-form watch specifically.
- **Long-form -> Shorts (the clip-support direction):** the VidCon Short is real, direct evidence this already happens — a real long-form/live moment gets repackaged and pulls its own real, separate audience (15.6K) beyond whoever watched the original long-form/live content.
- **Real gap:** this pass did not isolate real subscriber-conversion rate specifically from Shorts (as distinct from long-form) — Studio's subscriber-delta figures pulled this session were channel-wide and video-specific for long-form, not Shorts-specific. Flagged as a real next step rather than estimated.

The one real long-form-support Short in this sample came from a genuine, unscripted, real-time moment ("Scarlett accidentally did this in front of them," live at VidCon) — consistent with the whole project's standing content-authenticity discipline: the real, unscripted moment is the clip-worthy material, not a manufactured one. ## Recommendations, stated plainly

1. **Keep both real formats running** — they're not competing for the same real slot; the POV-skit format is a cheap, repeatable, original-content engine, and the long-form-support clip format is a real, working way to extend a big real moment (like VidCon) past its original video.
2. **Title/hashtag for real search, not just trend-chasing** — the discovery data says search and playlists are doing more real work than the Shorts feed itself; keep real, specific, searchable phrasing in Short titles rather than optimizing purely for feed-trend hashtags.
3. **Close the real subscriber-conversion gap** — worth a dedicated future Studio pull isolating Shorts-specific subscriber conversion, once this is a live priority.

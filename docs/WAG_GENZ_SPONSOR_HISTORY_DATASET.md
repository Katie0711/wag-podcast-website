# WAG Gen-Z Sponsor History Dataset

**Purpose (per Katie, 2026-08-16):** a real, verified evidence base of which brands/categories/agencies actually buy advertising against WAG's exact target demo — Gen-Z, majority-female, meaningful teen (12-24) skew — so WAG's own revenue strategy is built on real market behavior, not a guessed prospect list. Feeds directly into future Revenue Intelligence work and eventual HubSpot prospecting (see `WAG_REVENUE_PARTNERSHIPS_CONSOLIDATED_ARCHITECTURE.md`, `WAG_PRICING_INTELLIGENCE_METHODOLOGY.md`).

**Standing rule for this doc, same as `COMPETITIVE_INTELLIGENCE.md`:** every row needs a real, citable source. No placement is invented. Where a real brand relationship is confirmed but the specific episode/date/CTA couldn't be pinned down, the row is included and marked **UNVERIFIED-DETAIL** rather than filled with a plausible-sounding guess. A thin or negative result (a show with zero findable sponsors) is reported honestly, not padded.

**Method:** started from the two shows WAG has already formally identified as direct comparables in `COMPETITIVE_INTELLIGENCE.md` (LOL Podcast, Rock Pod). The task brief also named Pucker Up, Dropout, and Therapuss as "already-identified" WAG comparables — a check of `WAG_PACKAGING_INTELLIGENCE.md` and `COMPETITIVE_INTELLIGENCE.md` found neither actually contains those three names; they are not yet in WAG's own docs. They were evaluated fresh here as candidates rather than assumed. Every additional candidate was demo-verified before being added — several real, well-known shows were evaluated and rejected below because the evidence didn't support a genuine demo match. This is deliberately a smaller, real dataset over a padded one, per Katie's explicit instruction.

## 4. Synthesis — repeat agencies / intermediaries (the real business-development finding)

Per Katie's framing: these repeat intermediaries matter more than any single brand name, because they represent a scalable point of contact rather than a one-off relationship.

1. **Megaphone (Spotify's podcast hosting + ad marketplace / dynamic-insertion infrastructure) — the single most-repeated intermediary found.** Confirmed as the underlying ad-tech/hosting platform for **4 of the 6 shows examined**: LOL Podcast (confirmed via [podnews.net](https://podnews.net/podcast/iasg4), "Hosted on Megaphone," "dynamic content insertion," IAB v2-certified), Therapuss ([feeds.megaphone.fm/therapuss](https://feeds.megaphone.fm/therapuss)), Anything Goes ([feeds.megaphone.fm/stupid-genius](https://feeds.megaphone.fm/stupid-genius)), and Hot Mess ([feeds.megaphone.fm/HMI2780986480](https://feeds.megaphone.fm/HMI2780986480)). This is real, structural evidence — not a guess — that Spotify's own ad infrastructure (Megaphone + the Spotify Audience Network) already sits underneath the majority of real advertiser relationships in this exact demo. A scalable finding: WAG doesn't need 30 separate one-off brand conversations if its own podcast inventory is hosted/monetized through the same sell-side platform four independent comparable shows already use.
2. **Libsyn Ads** — confirmed, named, real ad-sales representation specifically for **LOL Podcast**, WAG's own #1 identified direct competitor. Has a public advertiser-inquiry page with real demo data and a real list of past sponsors ([advertising.libsyn.com/TheLOLPodcast](https://advertising.libsyn.com/TheLOLPodcast)). This is the most directly actionable lead in the dataset: Libsyn Ads is a real, named company actively selling ad inventory against WAG's exact target demo today.
3. **SiriusXM Media** — confirmed real ad-sales representation for **Hot Mess** from its Aug 2023 launch through Feb 2025, reported by Variety and NBC News in coverage of the Alex Cooper/Unwell Network split. Real and named, though current (2026) representation of the show post-split is unconfirmed.
4. **United Talent Agency (UTA)** — appears in two separate, real, independent contexts: (a) general talent/brand-deal contact for Rock Squad/Rock Pod (`therocksquad@unitedtalent.com`, per their public bio), and (b) co-owner of **Ramble**, the UTA × Cadence13 network exclusively signing YouTube-star-hosted podcasts (found via Pretty Basic, which itself didn't clear the demo bar — see rejected candidates above). UTA repeating across two unrelated real relationships in this research is itself a signal worth logging as a BD contact point.

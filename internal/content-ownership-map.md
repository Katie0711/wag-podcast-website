# Content ownership: wildadventuregirls.com vs. thewagpodcast.com

Canonical copy lives at `wildadventuregirls-website/internal/content-ownership-map.md` (governs both repos). Mirrored here for visibility when working in this repo.

**Short version:** wildadventuregirls.com owns the episode archive, the 5 topic hubs, and the broader WAG ecosystem. thewagpodcast.com owns recurring formats (Guys Answer Questions, segments), games, deep single-episode resources, and original podcast-native material. No migration of anything already live. See the canonical doc for the full breakdown and the gray-zone rule.

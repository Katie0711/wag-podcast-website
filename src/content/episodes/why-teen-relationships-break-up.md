Bella just got dumped.

That's not a hypothetical setup — it's what actually happened right before WAG sat down to record this episode. So instead of skipping past it, they turned the whole thing into a real conversation about why teen relationships actually fall apart.

In this episode of the [WAG Podcast](/), Angelina, Scarlett, and Annabella — the real teen hosts behind the show, [meet them here](/hosts/) — talk through real breakup stories, the earliest signs a relationship is already over, cheating, and what actually happens after a breakup, instead of what social media says is supposed to happen.

Here's how it actually broke down.

## The First Signs a Relationship Is Already Ending

Before anything else, the girls get into the early warning signs — the stuff that shows up before an actual breakup conversation ever happens.

One specific sign gets called out directly: what the episode names "the phone red flag," a specific behavior around phones that they say is a real sign it's already over, not just a rough patch.

## Real Breakup Stories (Told by the People Living Them)

This is where the episode leans all the way into firsthand stories instead of general advice.

There's a $300 date that turns into a full disaster. There's a Reddit story the girls react to about a mom stealing her daughter's boyfriend — which is exactly as chaotic as it sounds. And there's a breakup where the guy ends things with a girl and then, afterward, gives her a kitten. Make of that what you will.

## Why Some People Never Get Over Their Ex

Not every breakup story here is dramatic. Some of it is just honest — including a real conversation about why some people stay stuck on an ex long after the relationship is actually over.

## Breakup Revenge Ideas (Don't Actually Do These)

The girls also get into the worst breakup revenge ideas people actually try — the kind of thing that feels satisfying for about five minutes and then makes everything worse.

## Glow Up Mode: What to Actually Do After a Breakup

Instead of leaving it at "here's what went wrong," the episode dedicates a real segment to what comes next — what they call Glow Up Mode. It's less about getting back at anyone and more about what actually helps after a relationship ends.

## What Girls Should Actually Look For in a Guy

The episode closes by flipping the question entirely: not what went wrong last time, but what actually matters going forward.

## So... Why Do Teen Relationships Break Up?

Between the phone red flag, the $300 date, the Reddit stories, and Bella's own very real breakup, one thing is consistent: it's rarely one big dramatic moment. It's usually a pattern of smaller ones, noticed too late — or noticed right on time, and ignored anyway.

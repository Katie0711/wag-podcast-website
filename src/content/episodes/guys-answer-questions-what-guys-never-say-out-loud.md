Guys Answer Questions is WAG's recurring format — a real guy, real questions, no script. Installment 1 was Ryan. This one is Aiden, a close family friend who's been riding along with the WAG family since Angelina met him during track season.

This page is the other half — the direct answers from everything else Aiden got asked, chapter by chapter.

## Do Guys Overthink Texting Too?

Yes, according to Aiden — texting overanalysis isn't just a girl thing. The episode gets into how much guys actually reread messages, second-guess a lack of reply, and read into timing the same way girls do.
